1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the RealChinese mobile application (the "App", available on both iOS and Android / Google Play) collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is developed and operated by an independent individual developer with no parent company or affiliates.

2. Data Controller

The data controller of the App is CHANGYI Liu. If you have any questions about this policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact: steadyhanddev@outlook.com

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Anonymous usage data. With your consent, we collect anonymous usage statistics via Unity Gaming Services (UGS) Analytics, a service operated by Unity Technologies. This includes: (a) app activity — pages/scenes visited, time spent on each page and word card, learning progress (words/sentences completed), and in-app purchase events (product identifier and success/failure status); (b) approximate location — your country/region and timezone, inferred from your IP address; (c) device and performance information — device model, operating-system version, language, app version, crash logs and diagnostics; and (d) device/advertising identifiers used for basic attribution. This data does not contain directly identifying information such as your name or email, and is transmitted to Unity Technologies over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection solely for analytics and product-improvement purposes.

3.2 In-app purchases. All purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) as the merchant of record. We do not collect or store your card number, billing address, or any financial account information; Apple/Google only return a transaction confirmation (success/failure and product identifier) to us.

3.3 Accounts. The App does not require account registration, and we do not actively collect your email, name, or phone number.

3.4 Store-platform data. Apple and Google, as the distribution and purchase platforms, may independently process standard device and transaction information (for example, OS version, language, and region) under their own privacy policies. This is separate from the Unity analytics data described in 3.1.

4. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to: improve the product experience, identify and fix issues, understand the learning flow, process purchases you initiate, and meet legal obligations. We do not use personal information for ad targeting and we do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Legal Bases

For users in the EU/EEA, we rely on the GDPR: analytics data is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); purchase-related processing is based on contractual necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)). For users in China, we process personal information under the PIPL with your consent. For users in Canada, we collect with consent under PIPEDA for reasonable purposes. California users are further protected under the CCPA/CPRA.

6. Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only with the parties strictly necessary:

  • Unity Technologies — receives the anonymous analytics data described in Section 3.1 (app activity, approximate location, device and performance information, and device/advertising identifiers) for analytics and product-improvement purposes.
  • Apple / Google — act as the merchant of record and distribution platforms for in-app purchases, and may process transaction and standard device information under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell personal information and have not integrated any third-party advertising SDKs. Except where required by law or necessary to protect the App and its users, we do not disclose your information to others.

7. International Transfers

Because we use global service providers (Unity, Apple, Google), your data may be processed outside your country/region (e.g., in the United States or the European Union). These providers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or other appropriate safeguards.

8. Retention

Anonymous analytics data is retained per UGS policy (typically up to about 25 months). Purchase records are retained as required by applicable tax and consumer-protection laws. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to: access, correct, and delete your personal information; withdraw consent; object to or restrict processing; and obtain a copy where data portability applies. To exercise these rights, email steadyhanddev@outlook.com. You may also disable analytics in the in-app settings (if provided). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out.

10. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and language learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (16 in the EU, unless local law sets a lower age). If you believe we have inadvertently collected a child's data, please contact us by email and we will delete it promptly.

11. Security

We use reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed 100% secure.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective date". We encourage you to review it periodically.
