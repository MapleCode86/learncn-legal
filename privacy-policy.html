1. 引言Introduction

本隐私政策说明 LearnCN 移动应用（下称"本应用"，适用于 iOS 与 Android / Google Play 平台）如何收集、使用与保护您的信息。本应用由个人开发者独立开发与运营，无母公司或关联实体。

This Privacy Policy explains how the LearnCN mobile application (the "App", available on both iOS and Android / Google Play) collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is developed and operated by an independent individual developer with no parent company or affiliates.

2. 数据控制者Data Controller

本应用的数据控制者为：CHANGYI Liu。如对本政策有任何疑问或需行使您的权利，请通过以下邮箱联系：steadyhanddev@outlook.com

The data controller of the App is CHANGYI Liu. If you have any questions about this policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact: steadyhanddev@outlook.com

3. 我们收集的信息Information We Collect

3.1 匿名使用数据。经您同意后，我们通过 Unity Gaming Services (UGS) Analytics 收集匿名使用统计，包括：访问的页面 / 场景、在各页面与词语卡上的停留时长、学习进度（已学词语 / 句子）、应用内购买事件（商品标识与成功 / 失败状态）。这些数据不含姓名、邮箱、电话等可直接识别个人的信息。

3.1 Anonymous usage data. With your consent, we collect anonymous usage statistics via Unity Gaming Services (UGS) Analytics, including: pages/scenes visited, time spent on each page and word card, learning progress (words/sentences completed), and in-app purchase events (product identifier and success/failure status). This data does not contain directly identifying information such as your name, email, or phone number.

3.2 应用内购买。所有购买均由 Apple（App Store）或 Google（Google Play）作为交易方处理。我们不收集也不存储您的银行卡号、账单地址或任何金融账户信息；苹果 / 谷歌仅向我们回传交易确认（购买成功 / 失败及商品标识）。

3.2 In-app purchases. All purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) as the merchant of record. We do not collect or store your card number, billing address, or any financial account information; Apple/Google only return a transaction confirmation (success/failure and product identifier) to us.

3.3 账号。本应用不要求注册账号，我们不会主动收集您的邮箱、姓名或电话号码。

3.3 Accounts. The App does not require account registration, and we do not actively collect your email, name, or phone number.

3.4 设备信息。分析平台与商店平台可能处理标准的设备信息（操作系统版本、语言、地区），通常以匿名、聚合形式呈现。

3.4 Device information. Analytics and store platforms may process standard device information (OS version, language, region), typically in anonymized, aggregated form.

4. 使用目的How We Use Information

我们仅将收集的信息用于：改进产品体验、定位与修复问题、理解学习流程、处理您发起的购买，以及满足法律义务。我们不会将个人信息用于广告定向，也不向第三方出售个人信息。

We use the collected information solely to: improve the product experience, identify and fix issues, understand the learning flow, process purchases you initiate, and meet legal obligations. We do not use personal information for ad targeting and we do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. 法律依据Legal Bases

对于欧盟 / 欧洲经济区用户，我们依据《通用数据保护条例》(GDPR)：分析数据基于您的同意（GDPR 第 6(1)(a) 条）；购买相关处理基于履行合同之必要（第 6(1)(b) 条）。对于中国用户，依据《个人信息保护法》在取得同意的前提下处理。对于加拿大用户，依据《个人信息保护和电子文件法》(PIPEDA) 在合理目的下经同意收集。美国加利福尼亚用户另受 CCPA/CPRA 保护。

For users in the EU/EEA, we rely on the GDPR: analytics data is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); purchase-related processing is based on contractual necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)). For users in China, we process personal information under the PIPL with your consent. For users in Canada, we collect with consent under PIPEDA for reasonable purposes. California users are further protected under the CCPA/CPRA.

6. 信息共享Sharing & Disclosure

我们仅与以下必要方共享信息：Unity（匿名分析处理方）、Apple / Google（购买处理与分发平台）。我们不出售个人信息，也未集成任何第三方广告 SDK。除法律要求或保护本应用与用户安全之必要外，我们不会向其他方披露您的信息。

We share information only with the parties strictly necessary: Unity (anonymous analytics processor) and Apple/Google (purchase processing and distribution platforms). We do not sell personal information and have not integrated any third-party advertising SDKs. Except where required by law or necessary to protect the App and its users, we do not disclose your information to others.

7. 国际传输International Transfers

由于我们使用全球服务商（Unity、Apple、Google），您的数据可能在您所在国家 / 地区之外（如美国、欧盟）被处理。相关服务商依据标准合同条款或其他适当保障措施处理数据。

Because we use global service providers (Unity, Apple, Google), your data may be processed outside your country/region (e.g., in the United States or the European Union). These providers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or other appropriate safeguards.

8. 数据留存Retention

匿名分析数据依 UGS 的政策留存（通常最长约 25 个月）。购买记录依适用税法与消费者保护法律留存。我们会在不再需要时将信息删除或匿名化。

Anonymous analytics data is retained per UGS policy (typically up to about 25 months). Purchase records are retained as required by applicable tax and consumer-protection laws. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed.
