Frequently asked questions

Is RealChinese free to use?

Yes. The Beijing prologue is free — follow a traveler from the airport to the hotel to a first bowl of noodles. Additional city chapters (the Forbidden City, Tianjin's Ancient Culture Street, Shanghai's Nanjing Road) are available as in-app purchases. There are no ads.

How does the pronunciation slider work?

Every line comes with natural audio from native speakers. Drag the slider to break a sound down piece by piece and see how characters, words, and sentences fit together — great for beginners getting a feel for pinyin and tones.

The audio isn't playing. What should I do?

Check that your device isn't in silent mode and that the in-app ambient/voice volume is turned up in Settings. If a line stays silent, please email us with the chapter and page number so we can fix it.

Do I need an internet connection?

You can start the app and play anytime. Some chapter content is downloaded on demand, so an internet connection is needed the first time you open a new city chapter.

I found a hidden collectible / a missing cat. Where do I report it?

Lucky you! Drop us an email with the chapter and page. We love hearing which easter eggs people find — and if you spot a scene that's missing one, tell us.

Do you have plans for more cities?

Yes — new city chapters are in the works. Keep an eye on the app and the App Store listing for updates.
